# Taxi Real-Time Analytics Design System

## Overview
High-tech real-time geospatial telemetry dashboard for New York City taxi and ride-hail fleets (Yellow Cab, Green Boro, FHVHV).

## Colors
- Deep space midnight backgrounds (`#020B18`, `rgba(10, 22, 40, 0.88)`)
- High-contrast neon accents: Cyan (`#00F5FF`), Sky (`#38BDF8`), Yellow (`#FACC15`), Green (`#22C55E`), Purple (`#A855F7`)
- Text hierarchy: High-contrast off-white (`#F1F5F9`) and Slate muted (`#94A3B8`)

## Typography
- Primary UI: `Plus Jakarta Sans` for clean, modern readability
- Telemetry & Data: `JetBrains Mono` for precise tabular figures and metric readouts

## Elevation & Depth
- Translucent frosted glassmorphism cards (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) with subtle cyan border lines
